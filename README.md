# Violin Play-Along (phone and tablet app)

Open https://willemseac-lab.github.io/violin-playalong/ in Chrome on your phone or tablet and choose **Install app**.
It works offline and keeps its songs on the device. The heavy work (lyrics, chords, melody) is done by the PC program;
songs move between the two as song files (WhatsApp, email) or over home Wi-Fi.

Instrument sounds: tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus Brosowsky (CC-BY 3.0). Sheet music: VexFlow. PDF: jsPDF.
